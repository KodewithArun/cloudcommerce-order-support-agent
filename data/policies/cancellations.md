# Cancellations Policy

## When cancellation is allowed

Orders can only be cancelled while the status is "processing". Support must check the current order status before confirming a cancellation.

Orders that are shipped, delivered, cancelled, or refunded cannot be cancelled. For shipped orders, the customer may wait for delivery and then request a return according to the Returns Policy.

## Refund timing

Approved cancellations are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds take 5-7 business days to appear after processing.

## Personalized and made-to-order items

Personalized or made-to-order items cannot be cancelled once production has started, even if the order still shows as processing.

## How support should handle a cancellation

Support should verify the order belongs to the requesting customer, check the order status, and confirm whether cancellation is still possible. If it is not possible, support should explain the available return or warranty options instead.
