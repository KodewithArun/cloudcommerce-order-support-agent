# Shipping Policy

## Processing and delivery

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping normally arrives within 3-7 business days after shipment, while express shipping normally arrives within 1-2 business days.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier delays, and incorrect addresses can extend delivery times.

## Tracking

A tracking number is available once an order ships. Support may share tracking information only for the customer's own orders.

If tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, support may open an investigation with the carrier.

## Lost or delayed packages

A package is considered delayed when it has not arrived within 3 business days after the latest estimate. Support should confirm the address and check with the carrier before offering a replacement or refund.

## Address changes

Support cannot change the shipping address after an order has shipped. If the order is still processing, support should confirm whether the warehouse can update the address before promising a change.
