# Returns Policy

## Return window

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. The 14-day period starts on the delivery date, not the order date.

Orders returned after the 14-day window are not eligible for a standard return. If an item arrived damaged or incorrect, the customer should contact support within 48 hours of delivery.

## Item condition

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories. Used, washed, or altered items may be refused or receive a partial refund after inspection.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, digital downloads, and opened personal care items cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## How to start a return

Support should confirm the order number, delivery date, and reason for the return. Once eligibility is confirmed, support provides return instructions. Refunds are issued after the item is received and inspected.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds take 5-7 business days to appear after processing. The refund is always sent to the original payment method.
