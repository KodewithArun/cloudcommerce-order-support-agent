# Warranty Policy

## Warranty coverage

Warranty covers manufacturing defects for 6 months from the delivery date. This includes defects in materials or workmanship that appear during normal use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, normal wear and tear, or damage caused by misuse or unauthorized repairs.

## Making a warranty claim

Customers should provide the order number, a description of the defect, and photos where possible. Support should confirm the order is within the 6-month warranty period before starting a claim.

## Resolution

Depending on the item and defect, the resolution may be a repair, replacement, or refund. If the same defect cannot be resolved, support may offer a refund according to the Refunds Policy.

## Warranty and returns

The warranty is separate from the 14-day return window. A customer outside the return window may still be covered if the item has a manufacturing defect within the 6-month warranty period.
